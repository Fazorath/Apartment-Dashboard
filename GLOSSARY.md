# Glossary

**Apartment**: the group of Roommates who share one chore list, identified by its apartment code. _Avoid_: home, household.

**Roommate**: a person who belongs to an Apartment and can sign in to it.

**Chore**: a recurring job the Apartment keeps track of, due again a fixed number of days after it was last done. _Avoid_: task (except when talking about code).

**Assignee**: the name a Chore is assigned to. It's free text and isn't tied to a Roommate's account.

**Display**: the read-only, always-on view of an Apartment's Chores, meant for a shared screen such as the living-room TV. _Avoid_: kiosk, TV mode.

**Pairing code**: the short, short-lived code a screen shows so a Roommate can connect it to their Apartment as its Display.

**Paired Display**: a screen a Roommate has connected with a Pairing code. It can see the Apartment's Chores without signing in and can't change anything. An Apartment has at most one at a time, and any Roommate can unpair it. _Avoid_: display link.
